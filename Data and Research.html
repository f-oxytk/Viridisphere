The Numbers Behind the Problem

Davao City gives the issue a local scale. These figures show what the city throws away, what collection systems cover, and where recovery infrastructure remains limited.

Davao By the Numbers

1,100 tons/day

Davao City generated approximately 1,100 metric tons of municipal solid waste per day in the study's 2024 baseline. That is a calculated equivalent of about 401,500 tons per year, not a separately measured annual total.

Source: Davao City scenario study (2026)

What Does Research Say?

Davao-specific and Mindanao-focused studies connect local waste conditions with climate impacts, plastic pollution, and better management choices.

Davao waste scenarios

A 2026 scenario study estimated about 1,100 metric tons of municipal waste per day in Davao's 2024 baseline. Its modeled diversion approaches reduced environmental impacts by roughly 50-70% in the scenarios examined.

Read the 2026 study

Integrated waste planning

Suh et al. analyzed Davao's integrated waste-management network. The study reported 50.08% biodegradable, 18.42% recyclable, 29.19% residual, and 2.31% special waste in its dataset.

Read Suh et al. (2022)

Plastic pollution in Southern Mindanao

A 2024 survey around Southern Mindanao documented 13,970 land-based debris items and 1,405 sea-surface debris items. Riverbanks had the highest average density at about 3.6 items per square metre.

Read Fuentes et al. (2024)

Reducing sachet impacts

Research on plastic sachet programs compared collection, recycling, co-processing, and refillable packaging. Refillable packaging produced lower impacts than business as usual in the scenarios examined.

Read the sachet study

Our Purpose

Viridisphere exists to close the gap between what people know about waste and what people actually do about it. Studies keep showing that most households understand recycling matters, yet improper segregation, illegal dumping, and open burning remain everyday realities in our communities.

We were formed to turn that awareness into action through community clean-up drives, waste segregation education, and partnerships with local barangays and schools that put simple, doable habits in front of people where they live.

Our purpose is threefold: to educate people on proper waste management practices, to advocate for stronger local enforcement of waste ordinances, and to build a network of volunteers who can carry these efforts forward long after any single campaign ends.

Viridisphere contributes to the following SDGs:

  • SDG 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities — Viridisphere helps create cleaner, safer, and more resilient communities through better waste management and community action.
  • SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production — We promote waste segregation, recycling, reuse, and responsible disposal to reduce unnecessary waste and improve resource use.
  • SDG 13: Climate Action — By discouraging harmful practices such as open burning, we support environmentally responsible habits that help reduce climate impacts.
  • SDG 14: Life Below Water — Our efforts help prevent plastic and other waste from reaching waterways and oceans, protecting marine ecosystems.
  • SDG 15: Life on Land — We reduce land pollution and help protect local ecosystems by encouraging better disposal and cleaner communities.
  • SDG 17: Partnerships for the Goals — Viridisphere works with schools, barangays, community members, and volunteers to strengthen collective action and long-term impact.

By connecting education with community action, Viridisphere aims not only to inform people about proper waste management, but to help make responsible waste practices a lasting part of everyday community life.

Don't just know.
Do something.

Turning environmental knowledge into everyday habits.

At Viridisphere, our main goal is to stop just talking about the waste problem and actually do something about it! We want to help people turn basic environmental knowledge into everyday habits.

By giving students and community members easy guides and real opportunities to help out, we can keep garbage off our streets and out of our rivers. Real change starts at home, and we're here to help everyone make that switch step by step.

Simple Things
You Can Do Today

You don't have to do anything huge to make a difference.

01

Sort Your Trash Proper Way

Separate your biodegradable food scraps, recyclables, and regular trash into different bins at home so it's way easier to recycle and dispose of safely.

02

Cut Down on Single-Use Plastics

Try bringing your own tumbler, eco-bag, or reusable containers instead of using plastic bags, water bottles, and sachets every day.

03

Try Composting

Instead of throwing away fruit peels and food leftovers, turn them into compost for your plants or garden.

04

Join Local Clean-Up Activities

Help out in neighborhood or school clean-up drives to stop plastic from clogging up our street drains and washing into the ocean.

05

Spread the Word

Talk to your classmates, friends, and family about proper waste segregation and show them how easy it actually is.

The best way to learn about proper waste management is to start practicing it.

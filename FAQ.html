Proper waste management starts with understanding what we can do every day. Explore the questions below and learn how simple habits can make a difference.

01 What is proper waste management? +

Proper waste management means reducing, segregating, reusing, recycling, composting, and disposing of waste responsibly. It begins where waste is created — usually at home, school, or work.

02 Why should I segregate my waste? +

Segregating waste makes it easier to recover recyclable and compostable materials while keeping different types of waste from becoming mixed together. In the Philippines, waste segregation is primarily expected to happen at the source.

03 What waste can I recycle? +

Common recyclable materials include clean paper, cardboard, plastic containers, glass, and metal. However, what can actually be collected may differ depending on your local recycling or barangay program.

04 What is composting? +

Composting is the controlled breakdown of biodegradable materials into nutrient-rich compost. Food scraps such as fruit and vegetable trimmings, along with certain garden waste, can be composted properly.

05 What should NOT go into my compost? +

Avoid putting materials that can contaminate the compost or create health and odor problems. Human waste, dead animals, and other unsuitable materials should not be added to ordinary household compost.

06 How can I reduce the amount of waste I produce? +

Start by refusing unnecessary single-use items, reducing unnecessary purchases, reusing items whenever possible, and choosing products with less packaging. Small changes in daily habits can reduce the amount of waste going to disposal.

07 Is burning my household trash okay? +

No. Open burning of municipal solid waste is prohibited under the implementing rules of Republic Act No. 9003. Instead, waste should be properly segregated and handled through the appropriate local waste-management system.

08 How can I help Viridisphere? +

You can join our clean-up activities, participate in educational workshops, practice proper waste segregation at home, reduce your use of single-use plastics, and encourage people around you to do the same.

09 Can students join Viridisphere? +

Yes! Students can contribute by joining community activities, helping spread awareness, assisting in workshops, and practicing proper waste management in their own schools and homes.

10 Where can I learn more? +

Follow Viridisphere for more waste-management guides, community activities, and opportunities to turn awareness into action.
